# United States Module Design Decision 6: State Collection Licensing

## Decision

The state Compliance Packs will declare, as the requirement values of [HiveAR Design Decision 11](../../hivear/HIVE-0011-collector-licensing.md), whether each state and the District of Columbia licenses, registers, or bonds collectors, which debts each requirement reaches, what brings a collector within it, and whether a collector without it may sue. The federal pack, compliance.us, declares no requirement, because federal law sets no general license for debt collectors (needs verification). This decision records the values that research found on 2026-10-11 by reading each jurisdiction's statutes and its regulator's pages. The requirements split by the regulatory nature that a debt type declares under [HiveAR Design Decision 9](../../hivear/HIVE-0009-debt-type-structure-extension.md): some reach consumer debts only, many reach commercial debts as well, and Florida registers commercial collection agencies under a statute written for commercial claims. Every value in this decision is a research finding that needs verification by someone with compliance standing, is educational reference material, and is not legal advice.

## Where This Decision Sits

HiveAR Design Decision 11 builds the authorization records, the requirement shape, and the check, and holds no values. This decision supplies the United States values as Compliance Pack content, each state's values belonging to that state's pack, such as compliance.us.wa for Washington, the same way [United States Module Design Decision 3](MOD-US-0003-federal-contact-times-and-call-frequency.md) supplies the federal contact values.

## What the Requirements Look Like

The jurisdictions differ on five points, each a part of the requirement shape of HiveAR Design Decision 11 (needs verification).

- **Debts**: some requirements reach only consumer debts, those incurred for personal, family, or household purposes. Others reach any claim, so an agency that collects only commercial debt needs the authorization. Wyoming excludes a collector solely of business debts.
- **Activities**: most requirements reach collecting for others, several reach buying debt in default, and a creditor collecting its own debts in its own name is usually outside unless it uses another name.
- **Triggers**: a debtor located in the state, as in North Dakota; a client or creditor located there, as Alaska's regulator and Louisiana's statute read; the collector located there; or any of the three, as in Nevada.
- **Out-of-state collectors**: several states exempt an agency that collects only by interstate communications, some only with a home license and a reciprocal privilege, and Washington and Oregon require a separate out-of-state license or registration instead.
- **Consequences**: in Alaska, Oregon, and Washington an agency cannot sue on a client's claim without alleging and proving its authorization. Elsewhere the statutes read set criminal or civil penalties, and Tennessee lets an agency cure and allows no civil defense based on the missing license.

Four rows say unclear: Alabama, Louisiana, Massachusetts, and New Mexico. Under HiveAR Design Decision 11 the check treats an unclear requirement as reaching the debt, so until someone with compliance standing settles those rows, a commercial debt in one of those states is held unless the organization records an authorization or an exemption there.

## What These Values Do Not Cover

- The conditions of each authorization, such as bond amounts, trust accounts, and remittance deadlines, which an authorization record holds as information and whose enforcement is an open question.
- Licenses for offices and individual collectors, which some states require, for the later versions of HiveAR Design Decision 11.
- Licenses of a city, such as New York City's, which sit below the state level that pack identifiers reach. How a pack for a city is identified is an open question.
- The procedure for an agency's suit on an assigned claim, such as appearing through a licensed attorney, which is Boilerplate Pack content for the legal module and not licensing.
- The conduct rules that some states attach to their licensees and that reach commercial debtors, which belong to the mechanisms for contact and conduct and to later decisions of this family.

## Status of the Content

> **Legal force**: state statutes, with regulations and regulator guidance in some states, as each row names. A regulator's form or web page is guidance and not law. All of this needs verification.
>
> **Effective date**: the statutes as published when read on 2026-10-11, several with amendments effective in 2025 and 2026. Pending bills were not tracked.
>
> **Verification status**: read from statute text, regulator pages, and in places mirrors of statute text on 2026-10-11, and not reviewed by counsel. A row marked medium or low rests partly on regulator lists, code indexes, or secondary sources.
>
> **Coverage**: consumer and commercial debts, as each row says, by the regulatory nature that the debt type declares.

## Implementation Phasing

**HiveAR v1 (MVP):** The organization-level requirement for each state, with the debts it reaches, its triggers, its exemptions for out-of-state collectors, and its consequence for suits.

**HiveAR v2:** Office and individual requirements and reciprocal exemptions, as HiveAR Design Decision 11 phases them.

**HiveAR v3+:** The conditions that the authorizations carry, once their open question resolves, and revisions as statutes change, each recorded with its effective date.

**Breaking change risk: LOW. The decision is pack content that configures a core mechanism and changes at the pack's own cadence.**

**Confidence: MEDIUM. Each row was read from the jurisdiction's own sources where they could be reached, but 51 jurisdictions were read in one day, four rows are unclear, statutes change, and no one with compliance standing has reviewed the values.**

## Implications For Contributors

These values live only in the state Compliance Packs. HiveAR Design Decision 11 and the core never name a state.

A change to a value records its source, its legal force, its effective date, and its verification status, as [Wax Design Decision 8](../../wax/WAX-0008-extensibility-construct-nomenclature.md) requires of Compliance Pack values.

A row marked unclear ships flagged with low confidence, and the check treats it as reaching the debt until it is settled.
