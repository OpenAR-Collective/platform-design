# Module Decisions

*The following decisions are specific to individual modules rather than to the HiveAR core. A module supplies the structure, behavior, and economics of one kind of debt or one capability, loaded only by the agencies that need it, and changes at its own cadence above the more stable HiveAR and Wax layers. Decisions here are numbered within each module. How a module composes, from a shared pool of primitives referenced by key rather than redefined, is the module composition model in [Wax Design Decision 33](../wax/WAX-0033-module-composition-model.md). Because module decisions often address areas with less settled practice, they carry a confidence level, High, Medium, or Low, signaling how firmly the decision is grounded and where community scrutiny is most wanted.*
