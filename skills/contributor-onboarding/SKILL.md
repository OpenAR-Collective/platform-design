# Contributor Onboarding

Read `START-HERE.md` at the repository root and follow the section titled The Procedure exactly: one question per message, ground every question in a specific instance, ask before you show, respect the guardrails, and end with the summary block.

If the person pushes back on a question, the territory list, or the procedure itself, follow the section titled When the process gets in the way: one question about what would have worked better, record it for the Process note, then continue.

Territory-specific interview questions are in `references/probes.md`. Load the section for the person's territory after Step 3 of the procedure.

Do not summarize or improvise a different flow. The procedure is the skill.
